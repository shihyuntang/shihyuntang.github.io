Welcome! This section hosts the companion animations and supplementary materials for my PhD thesis, Characterization of Young Active Stars for Identifying the Youngest Exoplanets.

These visualizations illustrate the synthetic spectra and surface reconstruction generated by the EXSPLOSYN modeling code.
